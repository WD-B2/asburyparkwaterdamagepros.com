Carpet water damage restoration is extraction, drying and cleaning of wet carpet and padding. Carpet fibers dry quickly on top, but the pad underneath holds water for days. If carpet in your Asbury Park home or office is wet, call us before you decide to rip it out. Many carpets can be saved when the water was clean and the response was fast.

Lift furniture legs onto foil or blocks to prevent stains. Remove small rugs and items sitting on the carpet. Do not walk through wet carpet more than needed, and keep a household vacuum away from standing water.

Whether carpet can be saved

Two questions decide it: how clean the water was, and how long the carpet stayed wet. Clean supply water that sat for under a day usually leaves a salvageable carpet. The pad is often replaced, since it is cheap and slow to dry.

Contaminated water changes the answer. Carpet that took in sewage or flood water is normally removed. The sewage page and flood page explain why.

How carpet is dried

The crew starts with water extraction using a weighted wand. The carpet is then lifted and stretched in some cases so the pad can be pulled out and the subfloor dried. Air movers push air under the carpet while dehumidifiers pull moisture from the room.

Moisture meters check the carpet, the pad and the subfloor. Drying follows the structural drying process. After drying, the carpet is cleaned and restretched.

ANSI/IICRC S500 guides these decisions. It classes clean supply water as Category 1 and says porous materials that stay wet can degrade into a worse category. That is the reason time matters more than the age of the carpet.

Common sources of wet carpet

Wet carpet usually traces to a burst pipe, a water heater, an appliance or a basement leak. In each case, stopping the source comes first.

Odors signal trouble. A musty smell a day or two after a wetting means moisture is still trapped, and the mold page explains the next steps.

Carpet over concrete behaves differently from carpet over a wood subfloor. Concrete holds moisture and releases it slowly, while wood subfloors can swell. The crew sets drying to match.

Stairs, closets and carpet that runs under baseboards need attention too. Water pools at low points and travels along tack strips. If carpet is glued down on concrete, lifting it takes more care, and the crew may cut it into sections. Keep pets and children off wet carpet until the crew says it is safe, and ask whether any treatment products were used, so you know what to expect when you return.

Insurance and carpet

Sudden water losses are often covered for carpet and pad. Flood and sewer backup usually need separate coverage. Read your policy and ask your insurer.

Carpet water damage questions

Can wet carpet be saved?

Often, when the water was clean and the carpet is dried within a day or two. The pad is usually replaced.

Why replace the pad?

Pad acts like a sponge and holds water, and drying it in place is slow. Replacing it is cheaper than the risk of mold.

How fast does carpet start to smell?

A musty smell can begin within a day or two. It means moisture is trapped.

Can I rent a carpet cleaner to extract the water?

For a small clean spill, it helps. For a soaked room, it leaves the pad and subfloor wet.

Is carpet covered by insurance after a pipe burst?

Often, for sudden losses. Read your policy and ask your insurer.
