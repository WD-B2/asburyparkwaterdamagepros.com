Have this ready

What to tell the dispatcher

  • The address, including the floor or unit.
  • Where the water came from, if you know, and whether it is still moving.
  • Which rooms are wet and what kind of floor, wall or ceiling is involved.
  • Whether you have shut off the water or power, and whether anyone is in the building.
  • Your insurer, if you plan to file a claim.
