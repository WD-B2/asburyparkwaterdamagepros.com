Why does my house smell musty and what are these dark spots?

A musty smell and dark or fuzzy spots on walls, ceilings, baseboards or under sinks usually mean a surface has been damp for a while. Mold needs moisture, a food source such as drywall or wood, and time. Remove any one of them and growth stops.

Do not scrub or spray bleach on large areas, and do not disturb it with a fan. Stop the water source, keep the area closed off and call for an assessment.

We do not make health claims about mold. If anyone in the home has symptoms, ask a physician. The New Jersey Department of Health publishes mold guidelines you can read for general information.

Where mold hides after water damage

Mold grows behind wallpaper and paneling, inside wall cavities, above ceiling tiles, under carpet and pad and in attics and crawl spaces. It follows water, so the key question is where the water went.

A previous pipe leak, an AC drip, a wet basement or a roof leak often explains a growth site. Remove the cause first or the mold returns.

Mold inspection and clearance testing

An inspection finds the moisture and the growth. Clearance testing after cleanup confirms the area is back to normal conditions. Many lenders, buyers and insurers ask for it, and having a written clearance report helps during a sale or refinance.

An independent tester has no stake in the cleanup result, which makes the report more credible. Ask who performs the testing and what they sample.

What to do before help arrives

  • Stop the water source first.
  • Keep the area closed and do not run fans across it.
  • Do not scrub or spray large areas.
  • Photograph the growth and write down when you first noticed the smell.

Prevention after water damage

Dry fast. The structural drying process exists to prevent mold. Wet materials that cannot dry are removed, and humidity is kept down.

Fix leaks promptly, use bathroom fans and keep indoor humidity moderate. Replace wet carpet and drywall that cannot be dried.

Ventilation and humidity affect how fast growth returns. A closed room with little air movement and high humidity holds moisture on cool surfaces such as exterior walls and window frames. A dehumidifier, working bath fans and a clear gap between furniture and walls keep air moving. After cleanup, keep checking the area for new staining or smells over the following weeks, and call again if either returns.

Insurance and mold

Policies vary. Many cover mold only when it follows a covered water loss, and many exclude it when caused by long neglect. Read your policy and ask your insurer.

Photograph the growth and keep inspection reports. We cannot promise any claim outcome.

Mold questions

Do you handle mold emergencies at night or on weekends in Asbury Park?

Yes. The line is open 24/7, and same-day response is offered when a crew is available. Stop the water and call.

My lender wants a clearance report. Can you help?

Yes. Clearance testing after cleanup produces a written report. An independent tester gives it more weight.

Can I just bleach it?

Bleach on porous materials does not remove the roots. Large areas should be handled with containment.

Is the musty smell enough to call about?

Yes. A musty smell means moisture is present, even if nothing is visible yet.

Does homeowners insurance cover mold?

Sometimes, when it follows a covered loss. Read your policy and ask your insurer.
