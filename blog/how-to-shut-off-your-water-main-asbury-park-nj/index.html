How to Shut Off Your Home Water Main

Knowing where the valve is saves minutes when a pipe fails. Find it and test it today.

Call (732) 352-9660

By the Asbury Park Water Damage Pros team. Updated 2026.

When a pipe bursts, the single most useful thing you can do is shut off the water. Many homeowners lose precious minutes because they do not know where the valve is, or because it is stuck. A few minutes of practice on a calm day turns a flood into an inconvenience.

This guide explains where main valves usually sit, how to turn them and what to do if one will not move.

Where to look

Common valve locations.
Building typeLikely spotNotes.
Basement homeWhere the line enters, near the meterOften on a front or side wall.
Slab homeUtility closet or near the water heaterMay be in a garage or outside.
Crawl space homeNear the crawl space entry or outsideCheck the access hatch.
Multifamily buildingUnit shutoff under sinks, building main in the basementAsk the manager.

How to turn it

  1. Find the valve, which is either a round wheel handle or a lever.
  2. For a wheel, turn it clockwise until it stops.
  3. For a lever, turn it a quarter turn until it sits across the pipe.
  4. Open a faucet at the lowest point to drain the pipes.
  5. Check that water stops flowing.

Do not force a stuck valve with tools. A broken valve makes things worse. If it will not move, close the street side shutoff if you know where it is and call a plumber.

Test it now

Turn the valve off and on once a year. Valves that never move can seize. If yours is corroded or hard to turn, ask a plumber to replace it before an emergency. A modern ball valve is easier and more reliable than an old gate valve.

Label the valve with a tag so guests, tenants or family members can find it fast.

What to know in Asbury Park

New Jersey American Water supplies water in Asbury Park. Ask the utility which part of the service line is theirs and which is yours, and do not operate the curb valve unless the utility tells you to. If water is surfacing outside before your meter, call the utility.

Neighbors in Oakhurst and Neptune should confirm with their own utility which supplier serves their address.

Source type: utility service page.

ANSI/IICRC S500, the published standard for water damage restoration, treats fast control of the source as the first step. Shutting off the supply quickly is what keeps a Category 1 clean water leak from degrading.

After the water is off

Open the lowest faucet to drain the lines and relieve pressure. Photograph the damage. Then call a plumber for the repair and a restoration crew for the water. The burst pipe page explains what happens next, and water extraction begins as soon as the supply is controlled.

Also shut off the water heater at its breaker or gas control if the water supply has been closed for long, so it does not run dry.

If you rent or live in an apartment

Ask your landlord or manager where the unit shutoff and building main are before you need them. Many apartments have a shutoff under the kitchen sink or in a utility closet. Tell your neighbors, since a leak in one unit can reach several.

Never open a shared utility room you do not have access to. Call the manager's emergency number and report the leak right away. Put that number in your phone today.

What to do while you wait for help

Open cabinet doors to let water drain and photograph the damage. Move valuables up and out of the water. Mop or wet vacuum small amounts of water, but stay out of anything that touches outlets. Keep children and pets away.

If the supply is off and the water is still standing, call for extraction. The faster the water is out, the more materials can be saved.

A household plan

Make sure every adult and older child in the home can find and close the main. Practice once together. Post a note by the valve that says which way it turns and who to call.

Keep a plumber's number and the restoration line in your phone. In a real emergency, you will not want to search for numbers. If you travel in winter, shut the main off before you leave and drain the lines, or ask someone to check the house.

If the main valve is in a spot that is hard to reach, move stored items away from it. A valve behind boxes or shelving costs seconds you do not have. Mark the path with a small tag or a note, and keep a flashlight nearby, because the valve is often in a dark corner of a basement or closet.

Finally, remember that turning the water back on needs care. Open the valve slowly, check for drips at fittings and open a faucet to purge air. If you hear hammering in the pipes or see fresh leaks, close the valve again and call a plumber.

Other shutoffs worth knowing

  • Toilet supply valves at the wall.
  • Sink angle stops under each basin.
  • Washing machine supply valves.
  • Water heater cold inlet valve.
  • Outdoor hose bib shutoffs inside the house.

Fixture shutoffs let you stop one leak without losing water to the whole house. Test them once a year too.

Questions homeowners ask

Which way do I turn the main valve?

A wheel handle turns clockwise to close. A lever closes with a quarter turn so the handle sits across the pipe.

What if the valve is stuck?

Do not force it. Close the curb shutoff if you know where it is and call a plumber.

Who owns the curb valve in Asbury Park?

New Jersey American Water supplies the city. Ask the utility which part of the service line is theirs, and call them if the curb valve needs to be operated.
