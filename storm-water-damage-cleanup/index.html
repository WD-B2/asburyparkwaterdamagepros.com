Storm water damage cleanup covers rain and wind damage that lets water into a building. In coastal Asbury Park, nor'easters, tropical storms and heavy thunderstorms can drive rain sideways through window frames, under doors and past damaged roof sections. Call the 24/7 line when water is coming in, and move people away from windows and wet ceilings.

If a tree or branch fell on the roof, stay out of the area under it. Place buckets under drips, lift valuables and photograph the damage. Do not climb onto a wet roof.

Storm water: roof, windows or the ground

Where the water came from decides the plan. Water through a damaged roof wets attic insulation and ceilings, and the ceiling page explains how that is handled. Water through windows and doors wets walls and floors.

Water rising from the ground or the street is flood water, which is Category 3 and is covered on the flood damage page. Storm drains and sewers that overflow bring the sewage backup problem with them.

Hurricane Sandy and what it means for flood coverage

Hurricane Sandy affected Asbury Park in 2012. The New Jersey Department of Banking and Insurance notes that standard homeowners policies exclude flood and that storm surge is treated as flood. Wind-driven rain through a damaged roof is a different matter, and many policies address it.

Which applies depends on how the water entered. Read your policy before a storm, and ask your insurer or agent how rain damage and flood damage are treated.

What cleanup involves

The crew documents the damage, then removes standing water with extraction. Wet insulation and drywall are checked and removed where needed, and the structure is dried with air movers and dehumidifiers. Moisture readings confirm the result, as described on the structural drying page.

Temporary covers over openings protect the building until a roofer or window contractor makes permanent repairs.

After a major storm, many homeowners call at once. Calling early, and giving the dispatcher a clear picture of what is wet, helps get a crew assigned.

Insurance and storm damage

Wind and rain damage is often covered when a storm damages the building and water enters. Flood is excluded from standard homeowners policies. Read your policy, and ask whether a named storm deductible applies.

Photograph everything before cleanup. We cannot promise any claim outcome.

Storm water damage questions

Do you handle storm water damage emergencies at night or on weekends in Asbury Park?

Yes. The line is open 24/7, including during and after storms. Same-day response is offered when a crew is available.

Is rain through the roof covered the same as flood?

No. Rain through a damaged roof is treated differently from flood. Ask your insurer which applies to your loss.

Should I cover my roof myself?

Only if it is safe and dry. Wet roofs are dangerous. A roofer can place a temporary cover.

How soon should I call after a storm?

As soon as water is inside. Early drying saves more materials.

Do you repair the roof?

No. We dry and repair the damage inside. A roofer repairs the roof.
