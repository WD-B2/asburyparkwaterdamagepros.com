When Asbury Park residents say West Side, they usually mean the part of the city west of the railroad tracks and south of Asbury Avenue. That is the area this page covers, and its edges are approximate. If your address is near a boundary, call and give the street name.

Water damage here starts the way it does anywhere in the city. A supply line lets go, a water heater fails, a roof leaks after a storm or a drain backs up. What changes the job is the building, because houses, small multifamily buildings and larger apartment complexes sit within a few blocks of each other and dry differently.

Streets and buildings on the West Side

Springwood Avenue is the historic commercial corridor of the West Side, and Springwood Park sits at the corner of Springwood and Atkins avenues. Neighborhood planning documents describe new housing and retail appearing along the avenue, so some buildings are new while others are not.

Planning maps for the southwest side also show several public and subsidized housing complexes next to privately owned homes. We have no reliable count of basements, crawl spaces or construction dates for the neighborhood, so this page does not guess. The structure in front of you decides the plan.

Storm water and drainage on the West Side

Asbury Park's own storm alerts name the Bangs and Ridge area and Memorial Drive among the places that normally flood in heavy rain. That is a city statement about particular streets. It is not a finding about every block of the West Side, and the alert introduces the list as examples.

In August 2014 the Asbury Park Sun reported that firefighters rescued two people from vehicles in the 1500 block of Bangs Avenue, that one household was evacuated from a flooded home and that most intersections west of Memorial Drive had flooded. We found no neighborhood-wide flood history beyond reports like that one.

Flood risk: what is known and what is not

Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status not verified. Asbury Park's 2020 hazard mitigation plan says about 20 percent of the city's land area lies in the Special Flood Hazard Area, but it does not break that figure down by neighborhood.

To check one address, search it on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center or ask the city floodplain administrator. A Zone X label does not mean water cannot reach a building.

Sources: Asbury Park hazard mitigation plan appendix (2020), Asbury Park Sun (August 13, 2014), City of Asbury Park alert (August 20, 2026).

If Water Is Still Spreading

Stop the source

Close the fixture valve or the main shutoff if you can reach it without stepping through water. In an apartment, tell building management right away.

Stay clear of electrical hazards

Do not touch outlets, cords or the panel while standing in water, and do not operate breakers from a wet floor. If the panel is wet, leave the area and call the utility or emergency services.

Keep people and pets out

Water from a toilet, a floor drain or outside flooding can carry contamination. Household fans and an ordinary vacuum are not the right tools for it.

Document and protect

Photograph the damage while it is safe to do so. Lift small items, paper and electronics off wet floors and leave heavy furniture where it is.

What the first day of work involves

A restoration job usually begins with water extraction, which removes standing water with pumps or extractors. Next, moisture meters check walls and floors, because water travels into places that look dry.

Structural drying follows, with air movers and dehumidifiers running until readings come down. Some wet materials, such as soaked insulation or carpet pad, are removed instead of dried. The published reference for this work is ANSI/IICRC S500, which classifies water losses and describes drying practice.

Insurance paperwork

Coverage depends on the cause of the damage and on your policy. A sudden pipe failure is treated differently from rising water, and the New Jersey Department of Banking and Insurance notes that standard homeowners policies exclude flood. Renters should ask whether their own policy covers belongings and whether the landlord's policy covers the building.

Photos, moisture readings and an itemized scope of work can help describe a loss. They do not promise that a claim will be paid, because your insurer makes that decision.

West Side water damage questions

What counts as the West Side of Asbury Park?

Locals generally mean the area west of the railroad tracks and south of Asbury Avenue, with Springwood Avenue as its main corridor. The boundary is informal, so give your street address when you call instead of relying on the name.

Is the West Side in a FEMA flood zone?

Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status is not verified. Parts of Asbury Park are mapped as flood hazard areas, and individual addresses differ. Check your address on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center.

My apartment is leaking from the unit above. What should I do?

Tell building management right away, move belongings out from under the leak and photograph the damage. Stay out of any room where a ceiling is sagging or water is near light fixtures, then call for help.

Can fans alone dry a wet room?

Fans can move air, but they do not pull moisture out of walls and subfloors. After a small clean-water spill they may be enough. Contaminated water from a drain or from outside flooding needs professional handling.

Will my insurance pay for the cleanup?

That depends on the cause of the damage and the terms of your policy. Read it, ask your insurer or agent and keep photos and receipts. Flood damage is usually insured separately from a burst pipe.
