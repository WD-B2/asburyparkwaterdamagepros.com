Ocean Grove is a community inside Neptune Township, not part of Asbury Park. It lies on the Atlantic between Wesley Lake on the north, which is the boundary with Asbury Park, and Fletcher Lake on the south. Its ZIP code is 07756.

That matters in practice. Permits, floodplain questions and emergency management for Ocean Grove go through Neptune Township. Calls go to the Asbury Park water damage line next door, and the Neptune Township page covers the rest of the township.

A historic district with its own rules of thumb

Ocean Grove was founded in 1869 by the Ocean Grove Camp Meeting Association. The district was listed on the National Register of Historic Places in 1976. Reference summaries of the listing say the association still owns the land and leases it to homeowners and businesses.

If you lease your lot or live in a historic building, ask the association and Neptune Township's construction office how repairs are reviewed before work starts. Old wood trim, plaster and floors can be costly to match after removal, so ask which materials can be dried in place.

Fletcher Lake, Wesley Lake and the ocean

Neptune Township's recommendations report describes Ocean Grove as its only oceanfront community, sitting roughly 10 to 25 feet above sea level, and says Fletcher Lake is prone to overtopping in heavy rain. Both lakes border the district.

On September 29, 2023, flooding along Fletcher Lake was severe, and the Asbury Park Fire Department rescued people from cars trapped in floodwater. Neptune and county crews pumped water from the lake into the ocean. The township also planned to give out cleaning kits for flooded basements.

Flood status in Ocean Grove: known and unknown

Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status not verified. Neptune Township's report discusses flood zones for the township as a whole and models storm scenarios, which are possibilities and not events. We do not present them as a history of Ocean Grove flooding.

Check an exact address on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center and ask Neptune's floodplain office what applies. Flood is usually insured separately from a burst pipe.

If Water Is Entering the Building

Do not wade in

Floodwater from a lake or the street can carry contamination and hide hazards. Stay out of it and keep children and pets away.

Stay clear of electrical hazards

Do not touch wet outlets or the panel, and do not operate breakers from a wet floor. If you smell gas or the panel is wet, leave and call emergency services.

Move what is safe to move

Lift small items and papers to higher shelves. Leave heavy furniture alone until the water is out.

Photograph and write it down

Take pictures of the waterline and damaged items, then list what was affected for your insurer.

Extraction, drying and decisions about materials

Flood water is cleaned differently from a clean leak. The job starts with water extraction, and wet carpet pad, insulation and drywall that sat in lake water are normally removed. The structure is then dried with structural drying.

Ask any company how it applies ANSI/IICRC S500, the published standard for water categories and drying. The basement cleanup page covers lower levels in detail.

Insurance, permits and rebuilding

Coverage depends on the cause and on your policy, and the New Jersey Department of Banking and Insurance notes that standard homeowners policies exclude flood. Photos, readings and an itemized scope can help describe a loss, but they do not promise payment.

Ask Neptune's construction office whether flood-elevation rules affect a major rebuild. Ocean Grove's neighbors across Wesley Lake are on the Wesley Lake and Lake Avenue page.

Ocean Grove water damage questions

Is Ocean Grove part of Asbury Park?

No. Ocean Grove is an unincorporated community within Neptune Township. It borders Asbury Park at Wesley Lake. Permits and floodplain questions go to Neptune Township.

Is Ocean Grove in a FEMA flood zone?

Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status is not verified. Mapped zones vary by address, so look yours up on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center or ask Neptune Township.

Does Ocean Grove's historic status affect repairs?

It can. Exterior work in a historic district and on leased land may involve review. Ask the Camp Meeting Association and Neptune's construction office before you start, and keep records of the damage.

What caused the 2023 flooding near Fletcher Lake?

After heavy rain on September 29, 2023, reports said flooding was severe and people were rescued from cars. We do not have a street-by-street list of affected homes.

Will my insurance pay for flood cleanup?

It depends on the policy and the cause. Standard homeowners policies exclude flood, and separate flood insurance applies. Read your policy and ask your insurer before work begins.
