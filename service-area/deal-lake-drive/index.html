Deal Lake Drive runs along the northern edge of Asbury Park and carries Route 71. On one side is Deal Lake, a 158 acre lake overseen by the Deal Lake Commission since 1974. On the other are apartment buildings and other multi-unit properties, and a senior apartment tower stands elsewhere on the north side of town.

That mix shapes the water damage calls from this stretch. A leak in a single unit can travel through ceilings and walls to the units below. Heavy rain can collect in parking areas and lower levels. This page covers both.

What happened on September 29, 2023

Heavy rain on that Friday flooded much of the Asbury Park and Neptune area. Reporting by the Asbury Park Press said flooding covered parking lots near apartment buildings along Deal Lake Drive, and that elevator shafts flooded at some of those buildings and at the Asbury Tower senior apartments.

By the next morning, Deal Lake Drive was still closed between Main Street and Grand Avenue and between Park Avenue and the Loch Arbour border. The Deal Lake Commission chair described every street as a river and said the rain fell in about six hours, unlike a 2005 storm that stretched over a week.

Lake levels and how they connect

The lake drains to the ocean through a flume the commission chair described as a pipe about 8 by 5 feet under the Eighth Avenue jetty. Asbury Park's hazard mitigation plan notes that sediment reduces storage in Deal Lake and that high water there can cause flooding at Sunset Lake.

None of this tells you whether your building will take water in a given storm. It explains why the city asks residents near the lakes to move cars before heavy rain.

How much is known about flood risk on the drive

Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status not verified. Asbury Park's 2020 hazard mitigation plan says about 20 percent of the city's land area is in the Special Flood Hazard Area, and it names Deal Lake as a flooding concern, without mapping individual buildings.

A building manager or association can tell you what flood coverage and flood protection the property has. For one address, use the FEMA Flood Map Service Center.

Sources: Asbury Park Press (September 30, 2023), Deal Lake Commission chair report (2023), Asbury Park hazard mitigation plan appendix (2020).

What to Do If Water Is Rising Around Your Building

Skip the elevator

If water is in the garage, lobby or a mechanical room, use the stairs. Elevator shafts and equipment rooms can hold water and live electrical gear.

Call building management

Report the water to management or the board first. Shutoffs, electrical panels and common areas are usually theirs to control.

Do not drive through standing water

Cars were trapped in floodwater elsewhere in the Asbury Park and Neptune area in 2023 and the fire department had to rescue people. Move vehicles before the storm if you can.

Document your unit

Photograph wet floors, ceilings and belongings when it is safe. Move small items to higher ground and leave heavy furniture alone.

Condominium, rental and association questions

Who pays for what in a multi-unit building depends on the governing documents and the policies. Many condominium associations carry a master policy while each owner carries a separate one. Ask your association and your insurer where the line falls before work starts.

Renters should tell the landlord in writing and keep copies. Photos, moisture readings and an itemized scope help describe a loss, but they do not decide whether a claim is paid.

Drying a multi-unit loss

After water extraction, drying has to reach wall cavities, subfloors and the space above a dropped ceiling. In a stacked building that can mean checking the units above and below the source, with the owners' permission. Water categories and drying goals are described in the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard.

Deal Lake Drive water damage questions

Where is the Deal Lake Drive area?

Deal Lake Drive runs along the northern edge of Asbury Park beside Deal Lake and carries Route 71. The lake also borders Loch Arbour, Interlaken and Ocean Township. Give your street address when you call so there is no confusion over which town you are in.

Is Deal Lake Drive in a FEMA flood zone?

Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status is not verified. Check the exact address on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center and ask your building manager about the property's flood coverage.

Water got into our parking level. Is that something you handle?

Water in a parking level or garage is a flood damage job. Stay out of it while the water is moving or near electrical equipment, and tell building management. Call to describe what you see.

Who is responsible when a leak reaches the unit below?

That depends on the cause, your lease or condominium documents and the policies involved. Notify management in writing, document both units and ask your insurer how it treats damage that spreads between units.

Should I use the elevator after flooding?

No. If water has reached the garage, lobby or mechanical rooms, use the stairs and wait for the building to confirm the elevator is safe. Elevator shafts flooded at some buildings in 2023.
