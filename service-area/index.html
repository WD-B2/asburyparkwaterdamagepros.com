Coverage

How ZIP codes fit in

A ZIP code is a delivery route, not a service boundary. The five town pages list the ZIP codes that belong to each place so you can match your mailing address to the right page. If your ZIP is not listed, call anyway, because the line serves the surrounding area too.

Neptune has two ZIP codes. 07753 is the street-delivery code, while 07754 is a post office box code. Belmar has 07719 and 07715. Oakhurst, Bradley Beach and Fort Monmouth each have one on their pages.

Reaching the dispatcher

What to say about your location

When you call, give the street address first and the town second. A street address tells the dispatcher which municipality you are in, which matters because Neptune Township and Neptune City are different places, and because the former Fort Monmouth spans three towns.

Then describe the water. Say where it came from, which rooms are wet and whether it is still moving. If you are in a multifamily building, say which floor you are on and whether neighbors below have been affected.

The dispatcher will give you the safe next steps while a crew is arranged. Same-day response is offered when a crew is available, and the dispatcher will tell you plainly what to expect instead of promising a time that traffic or weather could break.

Have your insurer's name handy if you plan to file a claim, and tell the dispatcher about anything unsafe, such as water near the electrical panel or a smell of gas. Safety questions come before everything else on the call.

If you are calling for a relative or a tenant, say so and give the on-site contact's name and number. Crews can only start when someone can let them in, so a reachable contact saves time once a crew is on its way.

First steps

What to do before help arrives

  • Close the main or fixture valve if you can reach it safely.
  • Stay out of water that touches outlets, cords or the electrical panel.
  • Lift valuables off wet floors and photograph the damage.
  • Leave the building if water is rising or you smell gas.

ANSI/IICRC S500 is the published standard for water damage restoration, and it is a fair thing to ask any company about before you agree to work.

Ready to call? Have your street address and the source of the water in mind.
