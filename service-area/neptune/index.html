Neptune Township is the town immediately south of Asbury Park, and our 24/7 emergency line covers it. If water is spreading in a Neptune home, shop or rental, call now. Close the main valve, stay away from wet outlets and let the dispatcher walk you through the first steps.

Neptune is large, so the right answer depends on the building. A beach block home faces different water risks than a house near the Route 66 corridor. The tools are the same: extraction, drying and repair.

Neptune Township is bigger than most people expect

The township stretches from the Atlantic to Garden State Parkway exit 100. It meets Asbury Park and Ocean Township on the north and the Shark River estuary on the south. Ocean Grove and Shark River Hills sit inside it as census designated places. Neptune City next door is a separate borough.

That size matters for water damage. Beachfront buildings, multifamily properties and houses with basements all face different risks, so no single failure explains every call.

If Water Is Still Spreading

Close the valve

Shut the main or the fixture valve. A flooding bathroom or laundry can drop water through two floors.

Mind the power

Do not wade through water that touches outlets. Cut power at the panel only if it is dry and easy to reach.

Raise your things

Lift electronics, paper and clothing to dry shelves. Pull small rugs off the floor.

Document it

Photograph the source, the wet areas and the damage for your insurer.

ZIP codes and what they mean for your address

Neptune uses ZIP code 07753 for street delivery. ZIP 07754 is a post office box ZIP and is not a place a crew can be sent. If you gave an insurer or a utility a PO box address, give the dispatcher your street address instead.

Ocean Grove falls inside the township, and we mention it here only as a neighborhood within it.

ANSI/IICRC S500 is the published standard for water damage restoration. Ask any company you call whether its crews follow it, and ask for moisture readings that show the dry goal was met.

What to do before help arrives

  • Close the main or fixture valve and tell neighbors below you.
  • Stay out of water near outlets and light fixtures.
