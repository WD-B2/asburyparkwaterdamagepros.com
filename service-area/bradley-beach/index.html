Bradley Beach is a small oceanfront borough just south of Asbury Park, and our 24/7 emergency line covers it. With about 0.6 square miles of land, homes sit close together, and one failed pipe or storm can reach several neighbors. Call now if water is spreading, and close the main valve first.

A small borough with close quarters

Bradley Beach packs houses, multifamily buildings and small shops into a very small area. Water that leaks upstairs finds its way through ceilings into units below, so ceiling damage is a common result.

Age of plumbing is another factor. Shared walls and shared drains mean a clog in one building can show up in the next, which raises the chance of a sewage backup.

If Water Is Still Spreading

Shut off the supply

Close the valve at the fixture or the main. In a multifamily building, tell neighbors below you.

Keep clear of wiring

Stay out of water near outlets and light fixtures. Do not touch a switch if a ceiling is dripping.

Move belongings

Lift furniture legs, papers and electronics onto dry surfaces.

Record the loss

Photograph the source, the damage and any water that reached a neighbor.
