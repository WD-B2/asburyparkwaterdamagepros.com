Why Belmar has its own flood story

The borough sits between the ocean and the Shark River, with Silver Lake and Lake Como inland. Monmouth County's hazard mitigation plan from 2020 lists projects for Silver Lake, including a 48 inch pipe under A Street, and for Lake Como.

A borough alert in October 2025 named Highway 35, the Shark River area and the oceanfront as flood prone areas. If you live in or near those areas, expect high water during strong storms and plan ahead.

If Water Is Still Spreading

Stop the flow

Stay clear of power

Do not wade through water near outlets or the panel. Cut power only if the panel is dry.

Lift valuables

Raise electronics, paper and clothing onto dry shelves or an upper floor.

Take photos

Photograph the water line and damaged items before anything is moved.

Insurance and flood in Belmar

The New Jersey Department of Banking and Insurance states that standard homeowners policies exclude flood and that storm surge counts as flood. Because parts of Belmar are flood prone, check the FEMA flood map for your address and ask your agent about a flood policy.
