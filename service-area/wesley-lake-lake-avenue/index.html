Wesley Lake forms the southern edge of Asbury Park, with Ocean Grove in Neptune Township on the far shore. Lake Avenue runs alongside it. The Wesley Lake Commission, made up of representatives from Asbury Park and Neptune, oversees the lake.

This page sticks to what has been publicly reported and explains what to do about wet basements, ground-floor spaces and apartments nearby.

Documented flooding near Wesley Lake

Reported events near Wesley Lake and Lake Avenue.

In 2023, the mayor said he had seen water reach Bond Street only twice before. One Lake Avenue business reported about four feet of water in its basement, and local reporting said pumping it out took roughly five days. Basements near the lake can take on a lot of water when the lake overtops.

The 2014 report also noted that at high tide the ocean can wash over the lake's spillway even with the control gate closed. Water can therefore come from rain, from the lake and from the tide at once.

Why this edge of the city floods

Asbury Park's hazard mitigation plan says Wesley Lake drains close to half of the city's land area, plus parts of Neptune Township, to the ocean through piping beneath the beach. It lists work on the outfall pipes and the automatic flood gate as needed. It also says newer development near the lake has undersized drains that back up.

After the 2023 flood, officials weighed options such as a flapper on the outfall pipe, a pumping station and higher lake walls. We could not confirm which of these have been built, so we do not describe the current protection.

Flood zones and the limits of this page

Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status not verified. The hazard plan says about 20 percent of the city's land area is in the Special Flood Hazard Area and marks Wesley Lake on its flood map, but this page does not place any particular building in a zone.

Look up an address on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center, and remember that flood insurance is separate from a standard homeowners policy.

Sources: Asbury Park Sun (August 13, 2014), Asbury Park Press (September 29 and 30, 2023), NJ Spotlight News (October 2023), Monmouth County press release (July 9, 2026), Asbury Park hazard mitigation plan appendix (2020).

Before and During a Lake Flood Warning

Move vehicles

During flood watches, the city has asked residents to park away from Wesley, Sunset and Deal lakes and to move cars to higher ground.

Stay out of flooded basements

Water in a basement can reach outlets, a panel or a furnace. Do not go down the stairs to look, and do not run a pump from a wet floor.

Treat lake water as contaminated

Lake and street water can carry sewage and runoff. Keep children and pets away from it and wear protection if you must touch it.

Photograph from a safe spot

Take pictures of the waterline on walls and of damaged items once it is safe. They help later with insurance.

Extraction, drying and what happens next

Flood water is handled differently from a clean supply leak. The first job is water extraction, and porous materials such as carpet and drywall that sat in lake or street water are usually removed. The rest is dried with structural drying equipment.

The flood damage page and the basement cleanup page explain the steps. For water categories and drying methods, the reference is the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard.

Insurance and records

Whether rising lake water is covered depends on your policy, and standard homeowners policies exclude flood, as the New Jersey Department of Banking and Insurance notes. Ask your insurer which policy responds to your loss.

Moisture readings, dated photos and an itemized scope can support a claim file. They do not promise payment.

Wesley Lake area water damage questions

Where does Wesley Lake flood in Asbury Park?

Reports from 2014 and 2023 describe water on Lake Avenue and in basements nearby, and the 2023 flood reached Bond Street. The lake is the border with Ocean Grove, so flooding can affect both sides.

Is Lake Avenue in a FEMA flood zone?

Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status is not verified. The city's hazard plan marks Wesley Lake on its flood map. Check an exact address on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center.

My basement filled with lake water. Can I pump it out myself?

Not safely while it is rising or touches electrical equipment. Pumping too fast can also stress a foundation wall. Call, stay out of the water and let the professionals handle contaminated flood water.

Was there flooding here in 2026?

Monmouth County reported flash flooding on July 6 and removed sediment from the west end of Wesley Lake afterward. We do not have a street-by-street account of that event.

Does homeowners insurance cover lake flooding?

Usually not. Standard policies exclude flood, and separate flood coverage applies. Your policy language and the cause of the water decide, so ask your insurer before you assume anything.
